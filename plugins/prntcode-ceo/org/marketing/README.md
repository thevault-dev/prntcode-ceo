# Marketing · 🟡 partly live

**Owns:** attribution and cadence. Marketing works out what actually sells, and keeps PRNTCODE in front of customers at a pace they welcome. It traces every order to the channel and message behind it, and runs one contact calendar across ads, email and WhatsApp, so customers hear from PRNTCODE often enough to remember it and never so often they tune out. Paid Social sits inside it.

**Skills (live)**
- [`prntcode-brand-formatter`](../../skills/prntcode-brand-formatter/SKILL.md): puts anything that leaves the company into the PRNTCODE visual brand (HTML, Excel, PDF, Notion).

**Inside Marketing, installed separately**
- `paid-social-manager`: Meta spend toward a 4.0 MER, on a daily rhythm (data pull, tracking check, budget proposals, morning digest; creative analysis and briefs weekly). Nothing executes without Khaled's numbered approval. Installed from an upload, not this repo.

**Planned**
- **Attribution**: ties each Shopify order to the channel, campaign and message that drove it, using the UTM guide, discount codes and WhatsApp chats.
- **Contact cadence**: one calendar of every customer touch across ads, email and WhatsApp, with a cap per customer and a quiet spell after each purchase.
- **Email**: welcome, back-in-stock and new-print flows, starting in Klaviyo (already paid for, for audience sync).
- **WhatsApp sales**: opt-in broadcasts to small segments, and one-to-one selling for VIPs, building on the concierge and the sold-out button.

**Dropped (2 Oct 2026 review):** `prntcode-content-planner` and `prntcode-idea-generator`. They're legacy social content generators and won't move into this repo. Switch them off in Customize → Skills when you no longer need them.

**Scorecard:** share of orders with a known source · revenue per customer contact · unsubscribes and mutes kept low.

**Connectors it will lean on:** Shopify, Notion, Meta; Klaviyo and WhatsApp Business once connected.

**Note:** `Needs Content` in the tracker is the social team's flag, not a Chief of Staff task signal.
