# PRNTCODE Competitor Reference

Last updated: April 2026

This file documents the seed competitor list for the PRNTCODE pricing workstream.
Use these brands as starting benchmarks, then expand independently for each product type.

---

## Seed Competitor List

### 1. 1309 Studios
- **URL**: https://1309studios.com
- **Category**: Abayas
- **Closest PRNTCODE Arm**: PRNTCODE Collections
- **Positioning**: GCC-based contemporary abaya label. Artistic prints, bold silhouettes,
  collector-friendly drop model. Worn by international names. Strong brand identity.
- **Pricing tier**: Mid-premium. Abayas typically AED 800–1,600.
- **Use for**: Abaya pricing; regional brand positioning benchmark.

---

### 2. Marpholio
- **URL**: https://marpholio.com
- **Category**: Abayas
- **Closest PRNTCODE Arm**: PRNTCODE Collections
- **Positioning**: Qatari founder-led abaya brand. Distinct personal aesthetic. Structured,
  fashion-forward silhouettes pushing modest wear into RTW territory.
- **Pricing tier**: Mid-premium. Comparable to 1309 Studios.
- **Use for**: Abaya and modest RTW pricing; GCC founder-led brand benchmark.

---

### 3. Damson Madder
- **URL**: https://damsonmadder.com
- **Category**: Print-focused RTW
- **Closest PRNTCODE Arm**: PRNTCODE Collections
- **Positioning**: London-based female-led brand. Bold prints, playful feminine silhouettes.
  Conscious production, small-batch drops. Strong creative overlap on print philosophy.
- **Pricing tier**: Attainable luxury. Dresses GBP 120–250 (AED ~570–1,190).
- **Use for**: Print RTW dresses, tops, skirts. Closest Western equivalent for print philosophy.

---

### 4. Baggu
- **URL**: https://baggu.com
- **Category**: Print-focused lifestyle
- **Closest PRNTCODE Arm**: PRNTCODE Collections + PRNTCODE Lab
- **Positioning**: Brooklyn brand with cult following. Signature seasonal prints across
  functional product categories. Print-as-brand-identity across multiple categories.
- **Pricing tier**: Accessible. Bags USD 12–48 (AED ~44–176). Not luxury.
- **Use for**: Accessories and lifestyle items ONLY. Note: Baggu is entry-level — use
  as lower-bound anchor, not primary benchmark for PRNTCODE's positioning.

---

### 5. Liberty London
- **URL**: https://libertylondon.com
- **Category**: Textile house
- **Closest PRNTCODE Arm**: PRNTCODE Lab
- **Positioning**: The benchmark textile house. Print archive, B2B fabric supply, retail,
  and collaborations. Long-term aspirational reference for PRNTCODE Lab.
- **Pricing tier**: Premium to luxury. Scarves GBP 95–350 (AED ~451–1,663).
- **Use for**: Print scarves, home textiles, fabric-led products. Upper-end anchor.
  PRNTCODE should price 15–25% below Liberty for same category.

---

### 6. Marimekko
- **URL**: https://marimekko.com
- **Category**: Print-first lifestyle house
- **Closest PRNTCODE Arm**: PRNTCODE Collections + PRNTCODE Lab
- **Positioning**: The strategic north star. Founded 1951 on print-first philosophy.
  Entire lifestyle universe across fashion, home, accessories — all led by named,
  collectible prints. Proof of concept for print-as-entire-brand-identity.
- **Pricing tier**: Mid-premium to premium. Dresses EUR 150–350 (AED ~608–1,418).
  Home items EUR 20–200 (AED ~81–810).
- **Use for**: All product categories. Primary benchmark for print-led brand positioning.

---

### 7. Bouguessa
- **URL**: https://bouguessa.com
- **Category**: GCC modest RTW
- **Closest PRNTCODE Arm**: PRNTCODE Collections
- **Positioning**: Dubai Design District-based founder-led label. Elevated modest fashion
  into premium RTW. Same geography and customer segment as PRNTCODE, but minimalist/neutral
  — opposite of PRNTCODE's bold print positioning. Key for pricing and stockist strategy.
- **Pricing tier**: Premium. Pieces typically AED 900–2,500.
- **Use for**: Dubai-market pricing calibration. Good upper-bound anchor for what the
  local attainable luxury customer will pay.

## Pricing Tier Reference (AED)

| Tier | AED Range | Examples |
|---|---|---|
| Entry | < 500 | Baggu, basics |
| Attainable Luxury (low) | 500 – 1,000 | Damson Madder, Gorman |
| Attainable Luxury (core) | 1,000 – 2,000 | 1309 Studios, Marimekko, Bouguessa |
| Premium | 2,000 – 4,000 | Liberty London, Noon by Noor |
| Luxury | > 4,000 | Out of scope for PRNTCODE |

PRNTCODE's **target zone**: AED 500–2,500 depending on product. Most Core pieces
should land AED 900–1,600. Hero pieces can extend to AED 2,000–2,500.
