# PRNTCODE Pricing Examples

Worked examples for the most common PRNTCODE product types.
Use these as sanity checks when pricing real products.

---

## Example 1: Printed Abaya (Core)

**Production Cost**: AED 300
**True Cost**: 300 × 1.4 = AED 420

**Target margin**: 72% (Core)
**Target price**: 420 / (1 - 0.72) = 420 / 0.28 = AED 1,500

**Competitor range**:
- 1309 Studios printed abaya: AED 950–1,400
- Marpholio abaya: AED 800–1,300
- Bouguessa RTW piece: AED 1,200–1,800
- Market range: AED 800–1,800 | Midpoint: AED 1,300

**Positioning check**:
AED 1,500 is ~15% above the midpoint but within Bouguessa range. For a print-led,
artist-designed abaya, this is justifiable. Consider AED 1,450 for slight
discount vs Bouguessa.

**Recommended Price**: AED 1,450
**Actual Margin**: (1,450 - 420) / 1,450 = 71.0% ✓

## Example 2: Printed Scarf (Entry)

**Production Cost**: AED 80
**True Cost**: 80 × 1.4 = AED 112

**Target margin**: 63% (Entry)
**Min price**: 112 / (1 - 0.60) = AED 280
**Target price**: 112 / (1 - 0.63) = 112 / 0.37 = AED 303

**Competitor range**:
- Damson Madder scarf: GBP 65 = AED 309
- Liberty small silk scarf: GBP 95 = AED 451
- Marimekko scarf: EUR 85 = AED 344
- Market range: AED 309–451 | Midpoint: AED 380

**Positioning check**:
Target price AED 303 is ~20% below the midpoint — prices us too cheaply.
Opportunity to price higher. AED 350 gives a 68% margin and still positions
us 8% below the midpoint.

**Recommended Price**: AED 350
**Actual Margin**: (350 - 112) / 350 = 68.0% ✓ (upgraded from Entry to low Core)

## Example 3: Printed Tote Bag (Entry)

**Production Cost**: AED 55
**True Cost**: 55 × 1.4 = AED 77

**Target margin**: 60% (Entry)
**Min price**: 77 / 0.40 = AED 193
**Target price**: 77 / 0.38 = AED 203

**Competitor range**:
- Baggu: USD 28 = AED 103 (too low — not right comp tier)
- Damson Madder tote: GBP 55 = AED 261
- Marimekko bag: EUR 79 = AED 320
- Market range (appropriate tier): AED 250–350

**Positioning check**:
Min viable price AED 193 is well below market range. This is actually an
opportunity — can price at AED 250 for 69% margin, or AED 295 for 74% margin.
Recommend AED 250 to stay accessible and drive volume as an entry product.

**Recommended Price**: AED 250
**Actual Margin**: (250 - 77) / 250 = 69.2% ✓

## Example 4: Hero Art Print / Wallpaper Panel

**Production Cost**: AED 200 (per panel/meter)
**True Cost**: 200 × 1.4 = AED 280

**Target margin**: 78% (Hero)
**Min price**: 280 / 0.40 = AED 700
**Target price**: 280 / (1 - 0.78) = 280 / 0.22 = AED 1,273

**Competitor range**:
- Scion wallpaper (per roll): GBP 55–85 = AED 261–404
- Sanderson: GBP 75–120 = AED 356–570
- Anthropologie Home print: USD 200–400 = AED 734–1,468
- Liberty print fabric (per metre): GBP 35–80 = AED 166–380

**Positioning check**:
Target price AED 1,273 for a panel is at the high end of the Anthropologie range.
This is justifiable only if positioned as a limited-edition artist piece,
not commodity wallpaper. Must be presented as collectible art print.
Adjust to AED 1,200 for psychological cleanliness.

**Recommended Price**: AED 1,200
**Actual Margin**: (1,200 - 280) / 1,200 = 76.7% ✓

## Flag Example: Over-Costed Item

**Production Cost**: AED 650
**True Cost**: 650 × 1.4 = AED 910

**Target margin**: 70% (Core)
**Min price (60%)**: 910 / 0.40 = AED 2,275
**Target price (70%)**: 910 / 0.30 = AED 3,033

**Competitor range**: AED 900–1,800 (comparable RTW)

**Flag**: ⚠️ COST PRESSURE
Min viable price (AED 2,275) exceeds the top of the competitor range (AED 1,800).
Proceeding would require pricing PRNTCODE above premium competitors, which risks
brand positioning as inaccessible.

**Options**:
1. Reduce production cost by 35%+ (to ~AED 420) — requires supplier renegotiation
2. Reposition as Hero with a very strong design story — justify AED 2,500+
3. Accept 50% margin temporarily (AED 1,800) with a plan to renegotiate
4. Hold the product for the next collection pending cost optimization
